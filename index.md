I am a PhD Candidate at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en) and [Polytechnique Montréal](https://www.polymtl.ca), supervised by [Prof. Amal Zouaq](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/amal-zouaq) in the [LAMA-WeST Lab](https://labowest.ca). My research focuses on **large language models** applied to the medical domain, with a particular interest in **information extraction**, **non-parametric memories** and **agentic approaches**. 

Before my PhD, I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science (NLP) at the [RALI](https://rali.iro.umontreal.ca), Université de Montréal, under the supervision of [Prof. Philippe Langlais](https://rali.iro.umontreal.ca/langlais/), on evaluation methods for open information extraction. That work led to [BenchIE^FL](/projects/benchiefl/), an information extraction benchmark published at ACL 2024, and to general work on **evaluation methodology for information extraction and text generation systems**, which remains a common thread across my research, from structured information extraction to hallucination detection.
